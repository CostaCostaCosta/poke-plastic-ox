# Route 29

## Snapshot

Seventy tiles of westbound Johto meadow between Oldale's back gate and Cherrygrove's beach, with a
two-door gatehouse in the middle and grass deep enough to hide a Sentret. Two trainers stand in it,
both optional, both Lv. 4–5: this is where the game first asks the player to fight another person.
The emotional note is *a walk with consequences* — no story, but a team that comes out different.

## Plot points

1. **Entering from the east.** The connection from `OldaleTown` (offset 6) drops the player beside the
   east sign. Cherrygrove is a long walk west and nothing interrupts it; step 1 was spent at Pallet.
2. **JOEY is the first real battle.** `(35,22)` `PoxRouteTrainer_Route29` runs
   `TRAINER_PLASTIC_OX_ROUTE29`: Sentret, Pidgey, both Nidoran, Mareep, Spinarak at Lv. 5. He boasts,
   loses, and already admits in his after-battle line that he should train more — the payoff of the
   Route 101 warning.
3. **ELLA is the second, and she is watching birds.** `(52,20)` `PoxRouteTrainer_Route29Ella` runs
   `TRAINER_PLASTIC_OX_ROUTE29_ELLA`: Hoothoot Lv. 5, Hoppip and Rattata Lv. 4. Hers is the only
   Hoothoot on the route in daylight; the night tables are where Hoothoot, Spinarak and Houndour live.
4. **The gatehouse is open and points both ways.** `(34,10)`/`(35,10)` warp into
   `Gate_Route29_Route46_hns`: an officer at `(3,5)` and a boy at `(9,4)` give directions only, and the
   north door leads to `MAP_ROUTE46_HNS`. No lock — a Stage-0 player can simply walk in.
5. **Route 46 loops back instead of forward.** `Route46_hns` warps to the gate and to
   `DarkCave_SouthSide_hns` at `(20,11)`, which warps on to `MAP_ROUTE2` at `(14,20)` — an early
   shortcut onto Cherrygrove's north road, not a climb toward Blackthorn. `Route45_hns` is stranded
   outright: no warps, no connections.
6. **What is on the ground.** A hidden ball at `(55,6)` (`Route29_EventScript_ItemPotion`,
   `FLAG_POX_HIDDEN_R29_POTION`) holds `ITEM_POTION`; a Lum apricorn tree sits at `(15,11)`.

**Must not:** mention Team Rocket, Bill, Silph or the convergence; tell the player Route 46 is the
future downhill road from Blackthorn; turn the gatehouse into a story gate; invent a rival here.

## Dialogue guidance

- **JOEY** is sixteen and performs confidence; his sentences are announcements. Losing makes him
  quieter and more specific — the only arc on the route. Never a joke about Rattata.
- **ELLA** is spare and observational: what the birds do, not who wins. **The boy** carries a lunch that
  is too heavy and is honest about his laziness; **the woman** is on holiday and has nowhere to be.
- Register: unhurried, outdoors, slightly windy. Long grass sounds; distant sea. Before JOEY is beaten
  the route is a challenge; afterwards it is a place to walk.
- The old man's unused line ("I used to give different directions. Habit is stubborn.") is the best
  voice in the file — if a third NPC is wanted, that is the one.

> JOEY: Six of them. I picked them myself. That's the whole speech — let's go.

> ELLA: Hoothoot watches while I walk. If she turns her head, I stop and look too.

> BOY: It's uphill the whole way and my lunch is heavy. I'm not lazy, I'm loaded.

> WOMAN: The wind comes off the sea sharp in the morning. Twenty minutes in and it's somebody else's
> weather.

- **Do not:** explain why Johto meadow borders a Kanto road; let anyone mention regions, professor,
  Rocket or the convergence; make JOEY cruel or ELLA a pushover.

## Inspiration

- **Gen 2 Route 29:** the first route with a gatehouse and a trainer, Joey's vanity, the Tuesday walker,
  the feeling of a road that exists between three towns rather than leading to an attraction.
- **A real ridge road in early autumn:** long grass leaning one way, a lunchbox, a bird of prey on a
  fence post, wind that arrives before weather does. The gatehouse: two counters, one stove, a
  hand-drawn road map on the wall, an officer genuinely only there for directions.
- **Conversion:** four jobs — boasting kid, birdwatcher, boy with lunch, holiday walker — and one
  official. Overheard topics: birds, lunch, the wind, who is faster. The map is never explained.
