# Route 44

## Snapshot
The long road out of Rustboro's east gate, ending at the mouth of Mt. Moon. `Route44_hns` is the last ordinary ground before the first dungeon: three fishing ponds, seven trainers, a berry slope, and one hiker in front of the cave asking to see a badge. It is the hinge of Act I — Roxanne behind, Rocket ahead — and its emotional note is *errand*: people out here with lunch tins and damp socks, not with a prophecy.

## Plot points
1. **Leaving Rustboro is a threshold, not a cutscene.** Rustboro's east road fires `PoxRegion_rustboro_route44_a` on `(39,9)`–`(39,11)` and lands the player at `(1,15)`; the return is the coord trigger at `(0,15)` (`PoxRegion_rustboro_route44_b`) to Rustboro `(38,10)`. Nobody announces the stage change; wind and puddles do.
2. **The Stone Badge gate is the route's only story function.** Hiker `PoxRoute44_MtMoonGate` at `(68,13)` says Mt. Moon is rough going and that Roxanne's badge is required. That is the right way to make Gym 1 mandatory: a local man with a rule, not a barrier of light.
3. **The gate is not enforced.** The warp to `MAP_MT_MOON_1F` and coord trigger `PoxMoon_EnterFromRoute44` both sit on `(68,13)`, the same tile the hiker occupies, and neither checks `FLAG_BADGE01_GET`. Fix gate and tile together; do not ship a badge line that does nothing.
4. **Seven trainers, all Lv.13.** The first fisherman at `(11,5)` fields six Pokémon with Leftovers — the heaviest fight on the road — while the rest are two and three mon parties. Density suits a stage-2 approach, but the opening fight should be the mildest, not the biggest.
5. **Fishing is the texture and the balance risk.** The rod table offers Dewgong, Dragonair, Seaking and Octillery at Lv.10–13; two of those are a mid-game spike delivered before Gym 2. Decide on purpose.
6. **Reveal ladder: step 2 begins past this map.** Nothing here may mention fossils, receivers, unusual equipment, or Rocket. The road knows water, weather, and a mountain it does not enter.
7. **Must not:** open Mt. Moon without the Stone Badge, stage a Rocket encounter, use "convergence", or let anyone explain that the mountain is new.

## Dialogue guidance
- **Register:** cold water, warm lunch. Everyone is doing a small job and would rather discuss gear than the world. The gate hiker is the only authority here: bored, polite, immovable, and he still means the rule.
- **Fishermen** compare spots, bait and patience; they are the only people not in a hurry. **The scientist** treats the moved roads as a cartography chore. Before the badge the road is an errand; after it the same people are still standing there, which is the point.

> GATE HIKER: Rough going up there. I'll want to see the STONE BADGE before you try it. Nothing personal — I've carried people back down.

> LOST NAVIGATOR: My old map has no RUSTBORO west of this road. I'll pencil it in. No sense throwing good paper out.

> FISHERMAN: Three hours, one bite, and it was a boot. Best afternoon I've had all week.

> CAVE WALKER: Laces first. Everything else second.

- **Do not:** mention Team Rocket, fossils, or strange signals; promise a reveal inside the cave; call the mountain new; or explain the geography of three regions.

## Inspiration
- **A Cascade trailhead car park in October** — laces retied, coolers, a laminated notice about bears: gives the cave walker and the gate ritual their props.
- **Japanese *tsuri-bori* fishing ponds** — rental rods, a scoreboard, a regular who has been there since morning: splits the three fishermen into rental clerk, lunch, and regular.
- **A harbour ice house** — crates, scales, a smell you notice before the building: use for the fishing spot's props.
- **Kanto Route 44's GSC tone** — long, sparse, one authority figure before the mountain: pacing only.
- **Conversion:** gate man, three fishermen, one commuter, one scientist. Six voices carry the road, each with a reason to stand there.

## Open questions
- Should the badge gate be hard (no entry without `FLAG_BADGE01_GET`) or soft (warning only)? Stage 2 assumes hard, or Roxanne is skippable by walking past a talker.
- Is the `(68,13)` stack intentional — NPC as the door — or was the warp pasted onto the wrong tile? Test by walking at it; if the hiker blocks entry, the fix is a tile move.
- Are Dewgong and Dragonair at Lv.10–13 on this rod deliberate? If yes they need a tier note; if not, re-cut the table before the route ships.
- Salac and Petaya are strong for Act I. Keep both daily trees, or move one deeper into the route so the reward is not at the entrance?
