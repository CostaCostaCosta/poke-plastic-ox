Story notes

Act 1 · 2 — Mt. Moon · full · drafted
Design source: plastic_ox/story/locations/mtmoon-cave-hns.md

Snapshot

The player crosses Mt. Moon between Rustboro and Route 33: wet ladders, a stalled hoist, Zubat in the roof, and a field scientist arguing with four looters over two stone tablets. The playable dungeon is the imported chain MtMoon_1F_Frlg → MtMoon_B1F_Frlg → MtMoon_B2F_Frlg; this note's own map, MtMoon_Cave_hns, is a retired HNS shell kept only to catch saves made in the old cave. This is Rocket I of exactly three appearances — theft, not conspiracy. Leave the player cramped and slightly reverent: the mountain is older than the argument inside it.

Plot points

  1. The mouth. Route44_hns (68,13) fires PoxMoon_EnterFromRoute44 and warps to MtMoon_1F_Frlg (18,37). No badge check, no Rocket sighting.
  2. The descent. 1F and B1F are corridors of ladders, ledges, and dead side rooms; let the player get lost once. The 12–14 wild table is the cave teaching its own type mix.
  3. Miguel at the tablets. Trigger (14,11) runs MtMoon_B2F_EventScript_MiguelTrigger: Miguel walks in, battles (TRAINER_PLASTIC_OX_MOON_SUPER_NERD_MIGUEL, Lv.15 Magnemite/Spoink/Baltoy), and sets FLAG_POX_STORY_MTMOON. His demand is domestic: we each take one, no being greedy.
  4. Two tablets, one each. Taking the ICE TABLET (13,7) or the ROCK TABLET (14,7) grants ITEM_ANCIENT_ICE_TABLET / ITEM_ANCIENT_ROCK_TABLET, removes both balls, moves Miguel onto the other pedestal, and sets the flag. Re-talking at VAR_POX_MTMOON_STATE 2 gives the seal line: ICE to REGICE, ROCK to REGIROCK.
  5. Rocket I. Four grunts — (12,20), (18,27), (35,12), (37,21) — are prying relics off the walls to sell. Competent, tired, and entirely uninterested in what the tablets mean: theft plus one wrong conclusion.
  6. Step 3 is missing. The beat needs Rocket equipment receiving a signal that is not theirs. Proposed, new flag required: one scanner in the camp that hisses when examined, and a grunt who insists it isn't ours. No explanation, no name, no second read.
  7. Out. MtMoon_B1F_Frlg warp 7 lands on Route33_hns (27,17); no gate on the way out (see route33-hns.md).

Must not: name Silph, Bill, the adaptive system, or "convergence"; explain the signal or name its sender; give the grunts a boss, plan, or speech; turn the tablets into fossil revival (they are seal keys, not specimens); claim the seals relate to the region's geography.

Dialogue seeds

MIGUEL: I labelled both pedestals before you arrived. That is what field work

Dialogue guidance

  • Miguel is a field scientist with a numbering system and no funding: labels, crates, who has the better trowel. A colleague who arrived first, not a rival.
  • The grunts are four people on a night shift — awed, angry, thinking about resale, complaining about dust. None says "Rocket" twice in a scene, and the cave has no narrator: scale is carried by the pedestals and by every grunt line mentioning the rock. Voices stay down; only Grunt 1 raises his.
MIGUEL: I labelled both pedestals before you arrived. That is what field work is — arriving early and writing things down.
GRUNT 4: You feel that? The ceiling is older than the town you came from. I'm not supposed to think about it. I'm supposed to carry it out.
GRUNT 2: Our scanner keeps spitting out a burst that isn't ours. The boss says interference. Interference doesn't know our call sign.
GRUNT 1: Blacklist you? Kid, I can't even get a transfer out of this cave.
  • Do not: explain the signal; say "convergence", "network", "Silph", or "adaptive"; make Miguel a Rocket plant; let the tablets talk about cloning.

Inspiration

  • Real caving: carbide-smelling air, surveyor's tape on rock, a chalk arrow at a junction, the temperature drop three steps in. Item placement and Miguel's labelling habit come from here.
  • Museum collection splits: two institutions dividing a find, accession numbers, foam cradles, a crate marked fragile — the one-tablet-each rule.
  • Gen I Mt. Moon: cramped ladder rooms and the sense of a maze with a prize. Keep the maze, cut the expository NPCs.
  • Night-shift labour and awe: a crew that wants to finish before dawn, paid by the crate, plus one worker who cannot stop touching the rock. That worker is Grunt 4, the only person allowed near step 3.

  • Is the tablet choice exclusive per save, or may a reload yield both?
  • Should Route44_hns → MtMoon_1F_Frlg require badge 1, or is the mountain walkable before Roxanne with Whitney's flag check as the only backstop?
