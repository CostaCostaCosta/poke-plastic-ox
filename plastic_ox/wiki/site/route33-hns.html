Story notes

Act 1 · 2 — Mt. Moon · full · drafted
Design source: plastic_ox/story/locations/route33-hns.md

Snapshot

The first daylight after Mt. Moon: a short green shelf of switchbacks, one hiker, one man waiting for a letter, and a road that drops into Goldenrod. The player walks out of the cave mouth at (27,17), fights the only trainer on the map, and takes the western edge into the city. Route 33 is a decompression route — its job is to let a cave-battered team breathe before Whitney — so its emotional note is relief with an undertow: the city is close, and it is much bigger than the cave.

Plot points

  1. Out of the mountain. MtMoon_B1F_Frlg warp 7 lands here at (27,17). No cutscene, no guide. Give the player three steps of grass and sky before anyone speaks; the light change is the beat.
  2. Anthony's exam. The hiker at (17,20) has sight 3 and TRAINER_PLASTIC_OX_PU_ROUTE33 (six Lv.17 mons). He is the route's only fight and its real function: a full party check on the way out of a cave where it is easy to arrive with four usable Pokémon. He must be beatable, not a wall.
  3. The road to Goldenrod. Three trigger tiles at x=0 (y=22, y=23, y=25) run PoxRegion_route33_goldenrod_a and warp to GoldenrodCity_hns (33,44). The authored requirement list for this trail is empty, so the road is open in every state; the only objective check on the Goldenrod side is Whitney's own FLAG_POX_STORY_MTMOON gate at the Gym.
  4. Randy and the letter. The hiker at (25,27) runs POX_PU_MailRecipient. Before Webster's errand exists he only says he is expecting mail; once the player carries Delibird with the MAIL, he takes the delivery, sets FLAG_POX_DELIBIRD_DELIVERED, and gives the bird away for good. This is the route's only warm beat and it rewards a return trip, not a first visit.
  5. The headbutt tree. A sign-posted tree at (8,17) runs PlasticOx_EventScript_HeadbuttTree; keep it, it is the only reason to walk the east side of the map.
  6. No reveal. Nothing here advances the ladder. Rocket stays inside the mountain; the letter has nothing to do with the mountain.

Must not: put a Rocket grunt, a fossil, or a signal on this route; make Randy a quest board or a mail-system tutorial; let Anthony's defeat line be shared with every other trainer in the build; make the road a story gate it is not.

Dialogue guidance

  • Randy moved here from Route 31 for the quiet and has not settled in. He over-explains, thanks people twice, and talks about the letter before he talks about himself. He is lonely, not pitiful, and he never asks the player to fetch anything.
  • Anthony is a fell runner with a stopwatch and strong opinions about pacing. He respects preparation, not talent, and he corrects himself mid-sentence. After the battle he stops coaching and starts gossiping.
  • The sign is the third voice: municipal, cheerful, slightly out of date. It should read like paint, not a quest marker.
  • Register: open air, long vowels, people who are in no hurry. Sentences are shorter after the cave than in Goldenrod, where everyone talks over each other.
RANDY: I moved up from ROUTE 31 for the quiet. The cave hums at night — I've decided I like it. Ask me again in winter.
ANTHONY: Steady pace. The mountain doesn't care how strong you are. It cares how early you started.
ANTHONY: You came out with six standing. Most people lose one on the ladders. Eat something before the city.
SIGN: GOLDENROD CITY — follow the road up into town. Mind the gravel on the last bend.
  • Do not: have anyone mention Team Rocket here; explain where Delibird came from; use the words "convergence" or "region"; turn the sign into a tutorial.

Inspiration

  • A mountain pass road: stone retaining walls, a spring box, a cattle grid, a post with reflective paint. Converts to the road's geometry and to Anthony's pacing obsession.
  • Rural postal relay: a red postbox, a carrier's scooter, a redelivery slip. Converts to Randy as a man who checks the box twice a day and admits it.
  • Gen 2 Route 33: short, green, ledge-heavy, a breather between a cave and a town. Keep the length; cut the temptation to add a second story NPC.
  • Fell running: a club that times itself uphill on Sundays and talks about weather the way other people talk about sport. That is Anthony's whole voice.
  • The city lights over a crest at dusk: a one-time line, not an arrival cutscene. One passer-by, one sentence, and the road keeps going.

Open questions

  • Should the Goldenrod road check FLAG_POX_STORY_MTMOON? Today only Whitney's Gym script enforces the Mt. Moon completion, which means the badge is gated but the road is not.
  • Move Randy's letter hook behind the Delibird gift so a first-time player is not asked about mail that does not exist yet, or leave the foreshadowing?
  • Retire the duplicate sign object, or move it a tile so the tile has one interaction?
  • Does the route need a third voice (a porter, a sign painter) or is one hiker plus one settler the right population for a 20-second walk?
