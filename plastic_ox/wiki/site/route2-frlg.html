Story notes

Act 1 · 0 — Opening network · full · drafted
Design source: plastic_ox/story/locations/route2-frlg.md

Snapshot

The live Route 2: a long climb from the Cherrygrove trail to the Rustboro seam, split at the Ilex gatehouses into two biomes. The player walks it in through Ilex and out toward Gym 1, building an LC team from eight Kanto species in the south grass and eleven Johto/Hoenn species in the north — a border road where the birds change halfway.

Plot points

  1. South half (y ≥ 51) is the Kanto verge. Lv.4–6 Mankey, Growlithe, Vulpix, Pidgey, Rattata, Ekans, Meowth, Spearow; Ben at (10,72) has three Lv.6. Generous, shallow grass for a first team.
  2. The gate rows flip the biome. North is Lv.7–10 Taillow, Skitty, Electrike, Bellsprout, Hoppip, Ralts, Magnemite, Spoink, Trapinch, Voltorb and Gulpin, with Hoothoot, Koffing and Gastly at night. Nobody explains it; the calls simply change.
  3. North half is the pre-Gym exam. Wade at (9,8) sends six Lv.9 — Venonat, Aron, Baltoy, Spoink, Ralts, Voltorb — the fight that proves one type will not carry Roxanne.
  4. The hiker at (8,0) is the only lock. PoxRoute2_RustboroGate turns the player back until FLAG_POX_STORY_ILEX is set, then vanishes and opens the Rustboro seam without a line of dialogue.
  5. Dark Cave is a promise, not a path. (17,11) leads into MAP_DARK_CAVE_SOUTH_SIDE_HNS and on to MAP_ROUTE46_HNS; v7 forbids an early Blackthorn climb, so verify it.

Must not: explain the biome seam; name Bill, Rocket or Silph; make Dark Cave mandatory; add a rival.

Dialogue guidance

  • Ben treats his team as neighbours; Wade counts his six and explains breadth without saying "coverage"; the hiker is plain and brief. The gatehouse keepers keep their broom-and-carpet register — domestic and bored. The seam is heard, not explained.
BEN: You came up the south trail? Then you've met my whole team. They live in that grass.
WADE: Six of them, no favourites. That's the only trick I know, and it keeps working.
HIKER: Roots, mostly. Roots, and one very smug PIDGEY.
  • Do not: say "convergence" or name the regions; make the seam eerie; reuse Ilex's tree imagery; give Ben a quest.

Inspiration

  • The climb out of a fruit basin. Japan's Yamanashi and Kofu valleys: orchards and warm grass below, larch and colder air an hour up, birdsong changing before the view does. Staff it with a courier cycling the climb, a berry forager, a kid counting bird calls and the keeper with a broom and a schedule.
