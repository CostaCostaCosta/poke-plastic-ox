Story notes

Act 1 · 1 — Rustboro and the early Cottage branch · full · drafted
Design source: plastic_ox/story/locations/rustboro-city.md

Snapshot

The first real city: stone pavements, a Devon tower with tinted windows, a Trainer's School that is mostly a hallway, and a Gym that smells of chalk dust and wet rock. The player arrives after Ilex Forest, clears Gym 1, and can then take the optional northern branch to Bill's Sea Cottage. Rustboro is where the game first feels civic — traffic, offices, people commuting — and that normality is the point.

Plot points

  1. Gym 1 — Roxanne, Little Cup. The first serious team-building check. The player should lose once, learn to build a full LC team on the nearby routes, and come back. Roxanne is a teacher first and a Leader second.
  2. Devon is ordinary. Devon makes running shoes, PCs, and parts. Nobody here implies Devon built, caused, or knows about the merged geography. A Devon employee can be proud of a product and bored of the word "synergy".
  3. The Sea Cottage branch opens. Route 24 north to Route 25 ends at Bill's cottage: grandfather caretaker, no Bill, first gift Eevee. It is optional and must stay optional (see route-25.md).
  4. Route 44 east is the Mt. Moon road. A guide points east toward the mountain; the Rocket arc starts there and not before.
  5. The city remembers being a Hoenn city. Petalburg-style woodwork, a Devon-sponsored school, and a coastline that is now also Johto's. Residents notice the mismatch in practical terms (deliveries, ferry schedules), never in lore terms.

Must not: have anyone in Rustboro explain the convergence, mention Bill's Champion status, or treat Devon as sinister.

Dialogue guidance

  • Roxanne is precise and encouraging, uses classroom vocabulary ("show me your opening", "that's a good answer") and never taunts. She should sound like someone who marks papers at night.
  • Devon staff are proud of mundane things: the torque on a hinge, the warranty on a PC. They are the region's least mystical people.
  • Schoolchildren are confidently wrong in specific, funny ways. They are the best place in the early game for type-chart comedy.
  • Commuters talk about the day, not the world: a delayed shipment, a detour, a cousin visiting from "the other coast".
  • Keep the register brisk. Rustboro sentences are shorter than Ecruteak's.
ROXANNE: You built that team on the road? Then you already know more than half my class.
SCHOOLBOY: Rock beats everything if you throw it hard enough. That's science.
DEVON CLERK: The parts came in from the west coast. Don't ask me which west.
COMMUTER: Third detour this month. At this rate I'll know the whole map before the city does.
  • Do not: let any NPC say "because the regions merged" as an explanation. Do not mention Silph, Saffron, or Bill's research.

Inspiration

  • Rustboro as a company town: a mid-sized Japanese prefectural capital where one employer dominates — the bakery knows the shift schedule, the school has a Devon logo on the gym wall, the bus is always late.
  • Hoenn's Rustboro in RSE: cut-stone streets, a Devon tower you cannot enter for most of the game, and a Gym that feels like a geology classroom.
  • Cottage-country England for the branch: the north road narrowing into hedges, a cottage with a nameplate, a grandfather who offers tea before Pokémon.
  • Dialogue conversion: give each city NPC a commute or an errand. The map's oddness shows up as logistics ("the lorry came from the wrong coast"), which is funnier and more human than lore.
