Story notes

Act 1 · 0 — Opening network · supporting · drafted
Design source: plastic_ox/story/locations/route2-hns.md

Snapshot

The abandoned draft of Route 2: the same 24×80 corridor the player climbs from Cherrygrove to Rustboro, kept as a second map nothing can reach. Route2_hns (MAP_ROUTE2_HNS) has no connections, warps or coord events, and plastic_ox/alpha/region_v7.py lists it in the retired set; what survives is scenery, two signs describing a replaced world, and a hidden Revive nobody can collect. An archive record, not a scene.

Plot points

  1. Nothing enters, and it is the pre-port draft of the live road. No script or map in data/ names Route2_hns except data/maps/map_groups.json; the player walks Route2_Frlg (MAP_ROUTE2), which reuses LAYOUT_ROUTE2_HNS and joins MAP_CHERRYGROVE_CITY_HNS to MAP_RUSTBORO_CITY.
  2. Its signs describe the earlier topology. Route2_EventScript_Sign2 at (14,12) reads "VIRIDIAN FOREST ahead. Probably."; Route2_EventScript_Sign3 at (7,73) reads "South: RUSTBORO CITY. Yes, really." The live map puts a DARK CAVE sign and "CHERRYGROVE - RUSTBORO" on the same two tiles.
  3. The loot is stranded. Item balls at (20,55) and (17,72) both run finditem ITEM_POKE_BALL; the hidden item at (13,4) is an ITEM_REVIVE on FLAG_POX_HIDDEN_ROUTE2_19. The live map carries ITEM_ETHER and ITEM_PARALYZE_HEAL instead, so nothing here was meant to duplicate.
  4. Retirement is half-done. The generator empties connections, warps, coord events and PoxRegion_* objects and truncates Route2_hns_MapScripts to a bare .byte 0, but leaves the item balls, signs, hidden Revive and five unused text labels in place.

Must not: give this map a scene, an NPC, a quest, or an "odd presence" line. Do not treat its sign copy as canon, and do not wire a warp here — its coordinates sit inside the live Route 2 and the player would arrive with no way out.

Dialogue guidance

  • Nobody speaks here. The only authored text is two signposts and five orphan labels; there is no NPC, no trigger and no way in.
  • If kept as an archive loop, the register is civic and dry: people who maintain a road after everyone stopped using it — jobs, weather, a schedule, no interest in the player's errand. Sign copy names places that touch the live Route 2 — Ilex Forest, Cherrygrove, Rustboro, Dark Cave — never Viridian.

Inspiration

  • Paper roads. New Zealand and Ireland carry roads that were surveyed, exist legally, and were never built; walking one is a survey line through a paddock.
  • The A625 at Mam Tor. A British road closed by a landslip in 1979 and left as a right of way: signposted, walkable, quietly wrong.
  • Bypassed main streets. A town the highway went around keeps the wide pavement, the faded centre line and one shop that refused to move; the surveyor, the painter and the grazier using the verge staff this map.
