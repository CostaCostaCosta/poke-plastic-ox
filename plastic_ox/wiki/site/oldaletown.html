Story notes

Act 1 · 0 — Opening network · full · drafted
Design source: plastic_ox/story/locations/oldaletown.md

Snapshot

Four doors, four roofs and a crossroads: a 20×20 Hoenn village with a Mart, a Center, a footprint enthusiast, and roads leaving in three directions. The player passes through once on the way west to Route 29, takes a free Potion, and reads the north exit as "not yet" without being told. Its emotional note is first services — the first hint that this map is a network, not a line.

Plot points

  1. Arrival from Route 101. Down-connection from PalletTown_Frlg; otherwise only Route29_hns west (offset -6) and Route103 north. Nobody greets the player, and step 1 was spent at Pallet.
  2. The Potion handoff. OldaleTown_EventScript_MartEmployee at (13,7) gives ITEM_POTION once under FLAG_RECEIVED_POTION_OLDALE, with a bag-full retry and a reminder afterwards — the last free item before the first trainers.
  3. The footprints man. (8,9) LOCALID_FOOTPRINTS_MAN is gated on FLAG_ADVENTURE_STARTED: before it he blocks the path insisting he has found a rare Pokémon's prints; after it he admits they were his own. The town's single joke, and it works because he is entirely serious.
  4. The girl at (16,11) is a menu. OldaleTown_EventScript_Girl explains saving progress — an RSE leftover. The Center already teaches saving; she should be a person waiting for someone.
  5. The north road is a promise the town cannot keep yet. The up-connection opens onto Route103, an unrewritten RSE donor map with 22 objects, a vanilla May battle (TRAINER_MAY_ROUTE_103_TREECKO) and Birch's Pokédex-rating scene; it dead-ends, with no warps and no northern link. Version 7 later uses Route 103 for the Lavender→Cinnabar bypass, so this exit reads as "later", never as west.
  6. Two legacy actors are hidden, not deleted. The rival at (11,19) (LOCALID_OLDALE_RIVAL), hidden by FLAG_HIDE_OLDALE_TOWN_RIVAL, still runs the Emerald May/Brendan exit scene and writes VAR_OLDALE_RIVAL_STATE. Lance at (10,11), hidden by FLAG_POX_HIDE_BATTLE_LANCE (src/new_game.c), still holds a six-Pokémon ADV OU team (TRAINER_PLASTIC_OX_LANCE) and calls the player "P".

Must not: show either legacy actor in a normal file; run a rival scene; name Route 103's later role or the Cinnabar chain; explain the convergence; make the town motto ironic.

Dialogue guidance

  • The Mart employee is Oldale's closest thing to a narrator: he knows the delivery day, the stock, and which road the crates came in on. Practical, nosy, generous with a Potion.
  • The footprints man is a hobby naturalist with a plaster cast and no self-doubt: measurements, no winking. The girl is resting and allowed to be bored.
  • Register: flat, dry, domestic; sentences are short because everyone has said them before. Before the Potion the town is a corridor, after it a place with a person in it. Nobody knows the north road's future.
MART EMPLOYEE: Crates came up from the coast this morning. One of them was ours. That's never happened before.
FOOTPRINTS MAN: Four toes, no claw marks, and a stride like it was in a hurry. I've been sketching since dawn.
GIRL: I'm not lost. I'm waiting. There's a difference, and it's mostly sitting down.
SIGN: OLDALE TOWN — Where things start off scarce.
  • Do not: explain the junction; guess Route 103's destination; mention Bill, Silph, Rocket or the convergence; resurrect the rival or the Lance battle as "a bit of fun".

Inspiration

  • RSE Oldale: the first town with a shop and the scarcity motto; here in ORAS-style pastel hedges.
  • A Japanese roadside station (michi-no-eki): one shop, one bench, a noticeboard of roads you cannot take, vans idling, a clerk who knows every driver — and the amateur naturalist beside him with a footprint cast, bird list, camera bag and mud on his knees.
  • Conversion: three jobs — shopkeeper, hobbyist, someone waiting — plus the sign. Overheard: deliveries, mud, timetables; the merged world reaches them only as "the crates came up from the coast".

Open questions

  • Retire the rival object and its May/Brendan script outright, or keep it as a debug-only cameo?
  • The Lance battle: gate it behind the Champion flag as a rematch, or delete the object and keep only the trainer id stable for the metamon harness? The alpha trainer report argues for the id.
  • How should the north exit be blocked — an ordinary obstruction, or does Route 103 become a legitimate Act I detour once its donors are rewritten?
  • Is Oldale meant to be fishable at all, or is that table donor noise? And do the four interiors belong here, or do the Mart and Center want one shared note, since the same maps serve every Johto town?
