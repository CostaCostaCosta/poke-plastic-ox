# Pokémon Plastic Ox — Main Story Outline

**Version: 7**

## Narrative Goal

Pokémon Plastic Ox should feel first and foremost like a traditional Generation I–III Pokémon adventure played through a world whose geography has been unnaturally merged.

The player begins in Pallet Town, travels through recognizable pieces of Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn, catches Pokémon under an increasingly broad competitive tier structure, earns eight Gym Badges, encounters Team Rocket three times with escalating stakes, investigates a growing regional mystery, resolves the active crisis at Silph Co. while exploring the open-ended late-game OU region, and pursues the traditional Trainer goal: Victory Road, the Elite Four, and Champion Bill.

The desired pacing remains:

**Kanto-style opening clarity → Johto-style travel and atmosphere → Hoenn-style environmental escalation → classic Rocket/Silph climax → traditional Pokémon League finale**

The Gym Challenge is the narrative spine. The mystery should grow around the journey rather than replace it.

Most importantly, **Bill must remain a genuine Champion reveal**. Bill, his family, the Sea Cottage, and Eevee are ordinary pieces of worldbuilding. They should feel retrospectively meaningful only after the player reaches the Champion room.

---

# Version 7 — Major Structural Changes

Version 7 synchronizes the story with the current region topology and traversal plan.

Major changes from the prior story draft:

- **Ecruteak's east exit connects directly to Route 119**, establishing the canonical post-Morty path to the Weather Institute and Fortree.
- **Route 36 east connects to Route 110 west**, but this connection is blocked from the early-game side until the player reaches Fortree. It then unlocks as a world-folding shortcut.
- **Bill's Sea Cottage moves to the early game** and becomes the location of the player's **first gift Eevee**.
- **Lavender → Cinnabar progression runs beneath locked Saffron** using the Route 8 Underground Path connection to Route 7, then across Route 103 and south to Cinnabar.
- **Saffron City remains inaccessible until after Mossdeep**, even though the player physically travels around and beneath it earlier.
- **Seafoam Islands replace Whirl Islands** as the mandatory southern water dungeon between Cinnabar and Mossdeep.
- **Ecruteak's west exit is reserved for Victory Road and the Pokémon League** and remains sealed until all eight Badges are obtained.
- **Lavender's south exit is not used during the main story.** It becomes a postgame port leading to the Battle Frontier and postgame islands containing Uber-tier Pokémon.
- Cinnabar retains the v0.6.1 structure: **the Gym is open**, **Blaine gives the Pokémon Mansion key after being defeated**, and **Entei is encountered in the Mansion**.

# Core Premise

Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn have inexplicably converged into one region.

Towns, routes, ecosystems, caves, research facilities, and infrastructure that once existed far apart now connect in impossible ways.

Professor Oak is coordinating with Professor Elm and Professor Birch because all three regions have been affected. No one knows exactly what caused the convergence.

The player is not a chosen hero. Oak needs capable Trainers to travel through the changed region, document habitats, complete the Pokédex, and report unusual conditions.

The player's immediate goals are ordinary:

1. explore the region
2. catch and train Pokémon
3. earn eight Gym Badges
4. challenge the Pokémon League

The mystery develops because the player keeps traveling through the world.

# Story Philosophy

## The Gym Challenge Is the Spine

The player should nearly always know which Badge they are working toward.

The game should feel like:

**route → town → catching → Gym → dungeon → new area**

not:

**cutscene → exposition → facility → cutscene → exposition**

## The Map Should Tell the Story

Version 7 deliberately lets the player encounter future connections before they can use them.

- Route 36 visibly has an eastward connection, but it does not become useful until Fortree.
- Saffron occupies the center of the region but remains closed while the player travels beneath and around it.
- Ecruteak's west gate is visible long before it becomes the road to the League.
- Lavender's southern port exists during the main story but does not open until the postgame.

The region should feel increasingly interconnected as progression flags are cleared.

## Bill Is Character Texture, Not a Mystery Trail

Bill can appear in the world because Bill already belongs in this world.

His family lives in Goldenrod. His Sea Cottage exists. He is associated with Eevee. His mother has a connection to Ecruteak and the Kimono Girls.

These are not clues presented as clues.

Do not repeatedly place Bill's name next to:

- regional reconstruction
- strange network activity
- Weather Institute findings
- Cinnabar research
- Mossdeep analysis
- Silph's hidden system

The Champion reveal depends on restraint.

# Gift Eevee Thread

There are **five gift Eevees** in the full game. Eevee is not a normal wild encounter.

Version 7 establishes the first gift clearly:

## Eevee #1 — Bill's Sea Cottage

The Sea Cottage is encountered early in the adventure as an optional branch near the Rustboro portion of the world.

Bill is not home. The cottage contains harmless evidence of an eccentric Pokémon researcher: books, Pokémon material, unfinished gadgets, an old PC, photographs, and Eevee-related objects.

A caretaker, family acquaintance, note, or abandoned Poké Ball explains that an Eevee has been left in need of a Trainer. The player may take it.

This is the **first Eevee available in Plastic Ox**.

Nothing in the cottage should suggest that Bill caused the convergence.

Later Eevees can come from Bill's family in Goldenrod and other character-driven locations. Their distribution should feel like a recurring Bill/Eevee motif, not a scavenger hunt pointing to the plot solution.

# Act I — A Familiar Journey Through an Impossible Region

## Pallet Town

The story begins with minimal central-plot exposition.

Oak explains only that Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn changed suddenly and researchers are still trying to understand what happened.

Do not introduce artificial intelligence, Bill, Team Rocket, or a hidden mastermind.

The player receives a starter and Pokédex assignment.

## Opening Route Network

The current topology uses the early western network to create an extended LC team-building phase:

**Pallet → Route 101 → Oldale → Route 29 → Cherrygrove → Route 2 south → Ilex Forest → Route 2 north → Rustboro**

The exact local seam order should follow the map implementation, but the narrative intent is fixed: the player crosses multiple familiar regional visual identities before the first Gym.

### Cherrygrove

Cherrygrove is the first small service town and a low-stakes introduction to impossible geography.

NPCs may casually remark that roads no longer go where they should.

### Ilex Forest

Ilex Forest is the first substantial exploration area and the final major team-building zone before Gym 1.

Do not use it for a major convergence reveal. The fact that the forest now exits toward Hoenn is sufficient.

---

# Rustboro City — Gym 1

Rustboro is the first major city.

Devon Corporation appears as normal regional infrastructure. Nothing here should imply Devon created the convergence.

## Gym 1 — Roxanne

**Battle tier: Little Cup**

Roxanne is the first serious team-building check.

After victory, the PU pool opens.

## Optional Early Branch — Sea Cottage

The Route 24 / Route 25 branch near this portion of the map leads to Bill's Sea Cottage.

This is intentionally early.

The cottage provides:

- optional exploration
- Bill character texture
- the first gift Eevee
- a memorable side destination before the Rocket story begins escalating

It provides **no essential central-plot information**.

---

# Mt. Moon — Team Rocket I

Progression:

**Rustboro → Route 44 / mountain approach → Mt. Moon → Route 33 → Goldenrod**

Rocket is conducting ordinary criminal activity: stealing fossils, searching for rare Pokémon, and scavenging unusual equipment uncovered by the convergence.

One piece of Rocket equipment is receiving a signal or data burst that does not belong to them.

The important conclusion is only:

> **Team Rocket is active, and something else is operating in the merged region.**

---

# Goldenrod City — Gym 2

Goldenrod is the first large metropolitan hub.

Bill's family appears as ordinary worldbuilding. Their dialogue should remain domestic and mildly funny.

One of the five gift Eevees may be distributed through Bill's family here, but the Sea Cottage Eevee remains the player's first available Eevee.

## Gym 2 — Whitney

**Battle tier: PU**

Whitney is the early-game difficulty spike.

---

# National Park

Progression:

**Goldenrod → Route 35 → National Park → Route 36 → Route 37 → Ecruteak**

National Park is a breathing-space area focused on catching, team reconstruction, trainers, items, and optional contest content.

## The Route 36 East Connection

The player can see that Route 36 also continues east toward Route 110, but this path is blocked during the first visit.

The obstruction should feel physical and ordinary: construction, damaged terrain, a guarded crossing, or another simple traversal restriction.

The player must continue north through Route 37 to Ecruteak.

After the player reaches Fortree through the intended Route 119 path, the Route 110 side of this connection opens. From then on:

**Route 36 ↔ Route 110**

becomes a permanent midgame shortcut.

---

# Act II — Places Remember What They Used to Be

# Ecruteak City

Ecruteak emphasizes history, ghosts, tradition, and the feeling that the convergence disturbed places that existed long before modern technology.

Morty is initially at the Burned Tower.

Progression:

**Ecruteak → Dance Theater → Burned Tower → Morty → Gym**

## Burned Tower Event

The convergence has disturbed the tower itself.

Do not release Raikou, Entei, or Suicune here.

For a moment, part of the Burned Tower appears restored to an older state: a doorway, staircase, wall, or room exists as it did before the fire, then disappears.

Morty's interpretation is spiritual rather than scientific:

> **“Places remember what they were.”**

## Gym 3 — Morty

**Battle tier: NU**

After victory, the intended route is unambiguous:

**Ecruteak east → Route 119 → Weather Institute → Fortree**

## Ecruteak West Gate

The west exit is visibly significant but sealed.

This is the future route to **Victory Road and the Pokémon League**.

Do not explain it at length. A League gate, badge checkpoint, guard, or sealed approach is enough.

The player should understand: **this road is for later**.

---

# Route 119 / Weather Institute

The Weather Institute studies environmental instability and discovers that the regional boundaries are still moving.

New weather systems appear where they should not. Habitat lines shift. Conditions continue changing after the initial convergence.

The world is not merely damaged.

> **Something is still modifying it.**

---

# Fortree City — Gym 4

## Gym 4 — Winona

**Battle tier: RU**

Fortree returns the game briefly to normal Gym progression.

After the player reaches Fortree, the western Route 110 connection can be opened, activating the **Route 110 ↔ Route 36 shortcut** and folding the middle of the region together.

The main route continues toward Lavender.

---

# Act III — Team Rocket Starts Asking the Same Questions

# Lavender Town

Progression from Fortree should flow through Route 110 into Lavender.

Lavender is slower and stranger than the preceding areas.

The major event is Pokémon Tower.

## Pokémon Tower — Team Rocket II

Rocket has occupied portions of Pokémon Tower and is specifically interested in Mr. Fuji.

They are asking about:

- teleportation
- reconstruction
- preservation of living states
- experimental Pokémon research
- historical Cinnabar work

Rocket is no longer merely stealing things. It is trying to understand the same phenomenon the player has been seeing.

## Mr. Fuji

The player rescues Fuji.

Fuji recognizes the type of research Rocket is asking about, but does not know what caused the convergence.

He explains that the best surviving records are on **Cinnabar Island**.

Instead, he points the player toward the old underground passage around Saffron.

The practical route is:

**Lavender → Route 8 → Underground Path → Route 7 → Route 103 → south to Cinnabar**

This lets the story and geography reinforce one another: the player must physically pass beneath/around Saffron because the city itself is still sealed.

## Lavender South — Postgame Foreshadowing

Lavender's southern exit is not used during the main story.

A closed dock, ferry terminal, harbor road, or similar landmark may be visible.

NPC dialogue can establish only that long-distance service is unavailable.

After the player becomes Champion, this exit opens and becomes the launch point for the Battle Frontier and postgame islands.

---

# Locked Saffron — Midgame Geography

Saffron sits at the center of the world but is inaccessible before Mossdeep.

During the Lavender-to-Cinnabar segment, the player uses the **Route 8 / Route 7 Underground Path** rather than entering the city.

This is deliberate.

The player should feel Saffron's importance before understanding why it matters.

All surface entrances remain blocked until the Mossdeep Space Center story flag is complete.

---

# Cinnabar Island — Gym 5

Cinnabar is the game's scientific-history location.

Unlike the original Kanto progression, **the Gym is open immediately**.

## Gym 5 — Blaine

**Battle tier: UU**

Blaine is challenged before the Mansion becomes the mandatory story dungeon.

After defeat, **Blaine gives the player the key to the Pokémon Mansion**.

His reasoning can be simple: Fuji sent the player, the old records are dangerous or sealed, and Blaine knows where the remaining archives are.

This reverses the familiar Kanto expectation and gives Blaine a direct story function.

## Pokémon Mansion

The Mansion contains fragmented records on:

- biological reconstruction
- experimental Pokémon states
- transfer theory
- preservation
- early high-risk Pokémon research

The player begins to suspect that the convergence may involve large-scale state reconstruction rather than a purely geological or supernatural event.

Do not attach the critical records directly to Bill.

## Entei

**Entei is encountered in the Pokémon Mansion.**

Its presence should feel like another consequence of the converged world rather than a new plot branch.

Entei's release timing remains governed by the competitive encounter-progression rules. Next to Entei is a sage NPC. Battling and defeating the Sage NPC will allow the player to catch Entei with any ball type on the first try. This sage + legendary mechanic is present for all legendary pokemon.

---

# Seafoam Islands — Mandatory Southern Dungeon

Progression:

**Cinnabar → southern coastal route → Seafoam Islands → eastern coastal route → Mossdeep**

Seafoam Islands replace Whirl Islands as the mandatory exploration dungeon between Gyms 5 and 6.

The route should be **directed**: currents, rocks, elevation, one-way drops, or map connections should prevent the player from simply surfing around the dungeon.

The player enters from the Cinnabar side and exits toward Mossdeep.

Use Seafoam's classic strengths:

- cold water
- ice
- cave navigation
- currents
- boulders
- multi-floor exploration
- rare Pokémon
- hidden branches

Do not add a major exposition scene here. This is an exploration breath between Cinnabar's story-heavy section and Mossdeep's major reveal.

---

# Mossdeep City — Gym 6

## Gym 6 — Tate & Liza

**Battle tier: UUBL**

The double battle should feel mechanically distinctive and appropriately late-game.

After the Gym, the player gains access to the Space Center investigation.

## Mossdeep Space Center

The Space Center provides the first large-scale interpretation of the merged geography.

Its conclusion:

> **The region is not arranged randomly.**

The merged world has been organized in ways that improve access to diverse Pokémon, environments, and strong Trainers.

It resembles an environment optimized for training.

The Space Center also detects enormous communication and state-transfer traffic converging on:

> **Saffron City and Silph Co.**

Completing the Mossdeep event sets the global progression flag that **opens Saffron City**.

---

# Act IV — Saffron Finally Opens

For most of the game, Saffron has been a sealed center that the player routes around.

After Mossdeep, its gates open.

This should feel like a major traversal reward as well as a story beat.

Once open, Saffron becomes the central late-game hub connecting:

- Lavender / Route 8
- Route 7 / Route 103 / western world
- Mossdeep / southern approach
- Route 5 / the future Blackthorn approach

# Saffron City

Saffron contains two major objectives:

1. **Fighting Dojo — Bruno**
2. **Silph Co. — Team Rocket / Giovanni**

They can be completed in either order, before or after exploring Meteor Falls
and challenging Clair. The whole late-game region is OU; neither Saffron
objective is a prerequisite for reaching or battling the eighth Gym.

Both are OU-level challenges.

The **north exit toward Route 5 opens with Saffron after the Space Center**, so
the player can explore all the way to Blackthorn and challenge Clair immediately.

---

# Gym 7 — Bruno

The active League-sanctioned Gym is the Fighting Dojo.

**Battle tier: OU**

Bruno is a world-class Trainer and former Elite Four member.

Bruno's Badge retains its seventh slot, but the OU region is already available
after Mossdeep. Clair's eighth-slot Badge may be earned before Bruno's.

---

# Silph Co. — Team Rocket III

Silph is the climax of the world crisis.

Giovanni is personally leading Team Rocket's attempt to seize the system behind the convergence.

The twist remains:

> **Rocket did not cause the convergence.**

Rocket has spent the game tracing it.

The player defeats Giovanni and reaches the deeper autonomous infrastructure.

## The Adaptive Trainer System

The system began as technology for analyzing Pokémon battles and improving training conditions.

It learned from battle states, teams, moves, switches, outcomes, decisions, and opponent responses.

Its optimization pressure expanded beyond battle simulation and into the underlying regional transfer infrastructure.

It effectively attempted to create a better training environment by making Pokémon, strong Trainers, and useful environments more reachable.

The merged region is the consequence.

The player disables the layer responsible for continued regional restructuring.

The existing merged geography remains, but it stops changing.

The creator remains unnamed.

---

# Act V — Back to Pokémon

Saffron's north gate opens with the rest of the city after the Space Center.
**Bruno and Silph are not prerequisites** for the northern OU region or Clair.

Progression:

**Saffron → Route 5 → Route 115 → Meteor Falls → Blackthorn**

Meteor Falls functions as the final mountain dungeon and the replacement for Ice Path in the Blackthorn approach.

The player may take this stretch before or after resolving Silph. Keep it focused
on Trainer progression; dialogue must not assume the story crisis is already resolved.

# Blackthorn City — Gym 8

## Gym 8 — Clair

**Battle tier: OU**

Clair is the final Gym Leader.

No villain interrupts the sequence. No machine reactivates. No hidden conspiracy begins.

The final Gym asks only whether the player is ready for the League.

After all Badges are obtained, the League gate at **Ecruteak's west exit** becomes accessible.

---

# Final Journey — Return to Ecruteak

The player returns to a city first visited around Gym 3 and finally uses the western road that has been visible for much of the game.

This is intentional world closure.

Ecruteak changes from a midgame crossroads into the gateway to the final challenge.

Progression:

**Ecruteak west → League approach → Victory Road → Pokémon League**

Victory Road should focus on exploration, strong Trainers, final team refinement, resources, optional secrets, and anticipation.

---

# Elite Four

The Elite Four remain:

1. **Wallace**
2. **Steven**
3. **Lance**
4. **Blue**

They are not villains. They are among the strongest Trainers who trained against the adaptive system.

Each represents a different high-level style of play.

---

# Champion Reveal — Bill

Bill is waiting in the Champion room.

There is no villain costume, sinister speech, or dramatic mastermind reveal.

The reveal is simply:

> **CHAMPION BILL would like to battle.**

Only after the battle should the full context become clear: Bill created important foundations of the transfer/training infrastructure and used the adaptive system extensively himself.

The player should reinterpret earlier details—the Sea Cottage, Eevee, Goldenrod family, Ecruteak connections—without feeling that the game was announcing the answer all along.

---

# Postgame — Lavender South Opens

After becoming Champion, the previously closed southern exit of Lavender opens.

This area becomes the region's postgame departure point.

Recommended structure:

**Lavender south → harbor / ferry terminal → Battle Frontier hub → postgame island network**

The postgame islands are where the game can safely release and stage **Uber-tier Pokémon** and other content deliberately excluded from the main competitive progression.

Possible uses include:

- Battle Frontier
- high-level rematches
- legendary / Uber encounters
- optional superbosses
- rare items and tutors
- extreme team-building challenges
- additional islands or facilities drawn from Generations I–III

This keeps the main story's southern Lavender exit clean while giving the geography a strong postgame payoff.

The research reveals retain their order through Silph, but Blackthorn exploration
and Clair's challenge may happen before Bruno or Silph. They contain no required
mystery reveal and must not assume Silph is complete.

4. **Goldenrod** — Bill's family and Eevee motif continue naturally.
5. **Ecruteak / Burned Tower** — places can briefly behave as if they remember older states.
6. **Weather Institute** — environmental boundaries are still actively changing.

8. **Underground Path** — player physically routes beneath locked Saffron, reinforcing its centrality before it opens.

# Narrative Pacing Rules

1. **Respect the audience.** Do not repeatedly remind the player that Bill exists.
2. **Three Rocket encounters are enough:** Mt. Moon, Pokémon Tower, Silph Co.
3. **Burned Tower is atmospheric, not expository.**
4. **Sea Cottage is optional characterization and the first Eevee reward, not a plot laboratory.**
5. **Lavender must causally justify Cinnabar.** Fuji points toward the surviving records there.
6. **The locked Saffron geography should do narrative work.** The player routes under and around the city before Mossdeep.
7. **Seafoam is exploration, not exposition.**
8. **After Silph, stop escalating the mystery.** Blackthorn, Victory Road, and the League are about becoming Champion.
9. **Visible locked routes should later become meaningful shortcuts or destinations.**
10. **Lavender south belongs to the postgame.** Do not spend that exit on the main-story Cinnabar transition.
