# Pokémon Plastic Ox — Opening NU Encounters v2

**Progression**

Sudowoodo → Route 37 → Ecruteak / Kimono Girls → Burned Tower 1F → Burned Tower B1F → legendary-beast scene → Morty's Gym unlocks

**NU level cap: 30**

The design requirement is now:

> All 10 newly unlocked NU encounter lines must be obtainable before Morty's Gym becomes available.

This does **not** require all ten to be ordinary grass encounters. However, **Plusle, Dunsparce, and Chimecho are specifically ordinary wild encounters**, not gifts, trades, or statics.

---

# 1. Route 37

Route 37 introduces four NU lines:

* Roselia
* Plusle
* Murkrow
* Kecleon

Three are grass encounters; Kecleon is a Headbutt encounter.

This is the main outdoor NU-expansion area, while Burned Tower handles the more unusual species.

## Grass — Lv. 21–23

|     Rate | Morning     | Day         | Night       |
| -------: | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
|  **25%** | Growlithe   | Growlithe   | Growlithe   |
|      15% | Vulpix      | Vulpix      | Vulpix      |
|  **15%** | **Roselia** | **Roselia** | **Roselia** |
|  **10%** | **Plusle**  | **Plusle**  | **Plusle**  |
|      15% | Pidgey      | Pidgey      | Hoothoot    |
|      10% | Ledyba      | Hoppip      | Spinarak    |
|      10% | Spearow     | Spearow     | **Murkrow** |
| **100%** |             |             |             |

### Growlithe — 25%

Growlithe remains Route 37's primary species.

This preserves one of the route's strongest Gen II associations while avoiding the illegal early use of Stantler, which remains RU-gated.

### Roselia — 15%

Roselia fits the small, vegetated, Apricorn-lined route well enough to feel like an actual member of the ecosystem rather than a tier-placement obligation.

It is uncommon but not artificially rare.

### Plusle — 10%

This is now clearly the right solution.

Plusle's original Gen III acquisition is ordinary Route 110 grass, and HGSS specifically places Plusle on Route 37 through Hoenn Sound. Therefore Plastic Ox is effectively turning a later-generation conditional Route 37 population into a normal permanent population.

Plusle should remain 10% across all three periods rather than becoming a time-of-day gimmick.

### Murkrow — 10%, night only

Murkrow replaces Spearow at night.

This preserves the targeted nocturnal-hunting concept without turning Murkrow into another ubiquitous Dark-type encounter.

### Time-of-day structure

The route has a stable 65% population:

* Growlithe
* Vulpix
* Roselia
* Plusle

The remaining 35% provides the Johto time-of-day changes:

**Morning**

* Pidgey
* Ledyba
* Spearow

**Day**

* Pidgey
* Hoppip
* Spearow

**Night**

* Hoothoot
* Spinarak
* Murkrow

This is much cleaner than using time of day to store extra NU species.

## Identity

Route 37 Headbutt becomes the:

> **fruit-tree / woodland-mammal / camouflage population**

rather than another bird or bug table.

Teddiursa is the anchor.

Seedot and Exeggcute reinforce the Apricorn/fruit-tree environment.

Slakoth is arboreal.

Kecleon is the rare camouflage encounter hiding in the canopy.

### Kecleon — 5%

Kecleon remains an ordinary random encounter here, but rare enough that its later Route 119/Devon Scope appearance can remain its **signature canonical event**.

Plastic Ox does not need to pretend Route 37 is Kecleon's primary habitat.

This is simply its first NU-legal acquisition.

### Explicit exclusions

* Pineco: Ilex Forest exclusive.
* Aipom: Route 33 signature.
* Natu: Route 36 identity.
* Stantler: RU.
* no concentrated bug canopy.

# 2. Ecruteak City

Ecruteak itself should remain light on wild encounters.

No grass table.

No ordinary Headbutt table.

Its acquisition content is:

* Good Rod fishing
* Corsola → Relicanth trade
* Kimono Girls → Eevee gift

# Ecruteak NPC trade — Relicanth

## Traveling Hoenn fisherman

**Trade: Corsola → Relicanth**

Keep this exactly.

Corsola was deliberately introduced in the preceding PU block, so this creates useful continuity between tiers:

> obtain Corsola during PU → reach Ecruteak → optionally exchange one for Relicanth.

Relicanth absolutely should **not** become a freshwater Ecruteak encounter.

Its natural renewable habitat can still appear much later in the Hoenn Dive region.

## Recommended held item

Have the traded **Relicanth hold the Deep Sea Tooth**.

This elegantly solves another NU progression requirement.

The NU tier releases the Deep Sea Tooth for:

**Clamperl → Huntail**

Therefore one NPC interaction supplies:

* the new Relicanth line;
* the NU evolution item needed for Huntail.

The Corsola trade remains the actual acquisition cost.

---

# Kimono Girls — Eevee gift

The player battles the Kimono Girls before entering Burned Tower.

After defeating them:

> **The player receives Eevee.**

Recommended level:

**Lv. 20**

The gift should feel like a cultural/Eeveelution reward rather than a Pokédex-coverage transaction.

This also makes the Kimono Girls mechanically significant without forcing another city encounter table.

## Eevee progression implication

If this is Eevee's first availability, remove Eevee from normal LC acquisition and mark it:

> **Story-gated special exception — first obtainable in Ecruteak during NU.**

This is an intentional exception to the normal rule that LC-legal roots appear before Gym 1.

That exception is mechanically safe because Eevee itself is not what creates the relevant NU power spike; **Flareon** is.

## Fire Stone

The Fire Stone must also become obtainable before Morty.

Recommended placement:

> **Burned Tower 1F item pickup**

This ties the NU item release to the dungeon and gives the player the option to evolve the newly received Eevee into Flareon before challenging Morty.

Higher-tier Fire Stone evolutions remain tier-blocked.

# B1F population shift

## Koffing: 15% → 30%

This preserves the strongest canonical vertical change.

The deeper floor contains:

* poorer ventilation;
* trapped smoke;
* burned material;
* stagnant air.

Koffing becomes the floor's primary encounter.

## Gastly: 5% → 20%

The supernatural component becomes dramatically stronger.

This establishes the descent without replacing the entire ecosystem.

## Rattata: 30% → 15%

Ordinary urban vermin become less dominant downstairs.

## Zubat: 15% → 10%

Still present, but not defining.

---

# Dunsparce — 10%

Dunsparce must now be an ordinary encounter.

Its strongest Johto identity remains Dark Cave, where it was the defining rare Gen II catch.

Burned Tower B1F is therefore a deliberate ecological transplant.

The justification should be environmental rather than supernatural:

> Dunsparce lives beneath the tower, burrowing through old foundations and collapsed soil.

The player happens to encounter it on the lowest accessible floor.

Later Dark Cave/backtracking content can still provide a more canonical Dunsparce population.

At 10%, it is uncommon rather than frustratingly rare.

---

# Chimecho — 10%

Chimecho is also an ordinary wild encounter.

Its original Gen III habitat is the summit of Mt. Pyre, a location explicitly associated with Pokémon spirits.

Burned Tower is one of the few pre-Morty locations capable of preserving that ecological concept:

> old sacred place + death/history + spiritual activity.

Chimecho at 10% makes it meaningfully huntable without turning it into a primary encounter.

This is substantially better than the previous shrine-static proposal because Chimecho remains a traditional wild Pokémon.

---

# Sableye — 5%

Sableye remains B1F's rare encounter.

Unlike Chimecho, Sableye is primarily here for the physical environment:

* darkness;
* debris;
* inaccessible recesses;
* collapsed foundations.

Keeping it at 5% prevents the dungeon from becoming a generic Hoenn cave.

---

# 5. Burned Tower story event

After traversing B1F, the player reaches the legendary-beast scene.

Raikou, Entei, and Suicune appear only as scripted overworld/story entities.

They are:

* not battled;
* not catchable;
* not roamers;
* not counted as NU acquisitions;
* not unlocked for capture.

Suicune should linger briefly after Raikou and Entei depart to preserve the recognizable Gen II staging.

Completion of this scene causes:

> **Morty returns to the Gym and the Gym becomes accessible.**

Because every NU root has already been obtainable somewhere before this scene, the entire NU teambuilding pool is available before the Gym opens.

# 11. Burned Tower coherence audit

## Shared species

1F and B1F share:

* Rattata
* Koffing
* Zubat
* Gastly

That is enough continuity for the tower to feel like one ecosystem.

## 1F-exclusive emphasis

* Slugma
* Houndour
* Torkoal
* Raticate

This produces:

> exposed ruins / fire damage / urban scavengers

## B1F-exclusive emphasis

* Dunsparce
* Chimecho
* Sableye

This produces:

> buried foundations / stronger supernatural activity / darkness

The descent therefore changes **what dominates**, rather than replacing the ecosystem.

# 12. Canonical changes

## Plusle

This is now one of the least problematic additions.

Plusle is normally a Route 110 grass Pokémon and is specifically obtainable on Route 37 in HGSS via Hoenn Sound.

Plastic Ox merely makes that Route 37 association permanent.

## Dunsparce

Canonical Gen II home: Dark Cave.

Changed because Dunsparce must be available before Morty and must remain a normal encounter.

B1F is the best available approximation of its underground/burrowing ecology.

## Chimecho

Canonical Gen III home: Mt. Pyre.

Burned Tower deliberately substitutes one spiritually significant old ruin for another.

## Stantler

Still absent despite being iconic Route 37 fauna.

Reason:

**Stantler remains RU-gated.**

## Torkoal

Moved forward from its more obvious later volcanic habitat because all NU roots must be available pre-Morty.

Burned Tower's fire/smoke history makes this defensible.

## Relicanth

No ecological compromise.

Its natural deep-ocean habitat remains later; its early NU accessibility is supplied by a Hoenn NPC trade.

# 13. Final encounter pacing

The NU rollout now feels like:

**Sudowoodo**

> NU begins with a mandatory unusual static.

**Route 37**

> The outdoor ecosystem visibly expands: Roselia, Plusle, Murkrow, and rare Kecleon.

**Kimono Girls**

> The player receives Eevee and gains a new evolution project.

**Ecruteak fishing/trade**

> Relicanth becomes obtainable without corrupting freshwater ecology.

**Burned Tower 1F**

> Familiar abandoned-building fauna plus Torkoal establish the fire-scarred identity.

**Burned Tower B1F**

> Dunsparce appears around the buried foundations, while Chimecho and Sableye push the tower decisively toward the supernatural.

**Legendary beasts**

> The section ends on story and atmosphere.

**Morty's Gym**

> Every NU line is now legally available for team construction.

This keeps full pre-Gym NU availability without making Route 37 a Pokédex warehouse or turning Burned Tower into an unrelated collection of rare Pokémon.
