# Pokémon Plastic Ox — Pre–Mt. Moon LC Encounters v4.4

## v4.4 changes

- **Route 104 grass is reduced to six species** because the usable grass
  patch is very small.
- **Geodude drops from 15% to 5% in Dark Cave** because it is already a
  25% Route 46 encounter.
- **Bagon stays on Route 46 at 5%**; the optional side-route placement
  is intentionally rewarding.
- **Wailmer moves to Route 103 Old Rod at 5%** and is removed from Route
  44, making it a more canonical Hoenn backtracking reward.
- Magnemite moves from Route 104 to Dark Cave.
- Trapinch moves from Route 104 to Route 46.
- Voltorb moves from Route 104 to Route 103; at night its slot becomes
  Koffing.
- Gulpin is removed from Route 104 and remains on Route 103.

# 15. Route 44

Route 44 is intentionally a **backtracking map**. On the initial LC
visit, the central grass patch cannot be reached because the player must
Surf across the ponds to enter it. The shoreline can still be fished
with the Old Rod. Once Surf is obtained during the UU stage, the player
can reach the grass and its later-tier encounter population.

The later tables use the canonical Gen II Route 44 identity as their
base: **Tangela, Lickitung, Bellsprout, Weepinbell, Poliwag, Poliwhirl,
and Remoraid** are the core reference species. Girafarig and Kangaskhan
are layered into the Surf-gated grass rather than appearing as Surf
encounters.

preserving the two new UU targets.

Both are grass encounters; Surf is required only to physically reach the
grass.

Rhyhorn occupies the final 5% daytime slot as a callback to Route 44’s
Poké Maniac trainer and as a reasonable lower-tier rare encounter. At
night, that slot is absorbed by the stronger canonical water/grass
transition.

## Route 44 v4.4 implementation rules

1.  The grass encounter map exists from the start, but **the grass tiles
    are unreachable without Surf**.
2.  Girafarig and Kangaskhan are **walking/grass encounters**, never
    Surf encounters.
3.  Surf encounters remain strongly based on canonical Route 44’s
    Poliwag/Poliwhirl population.
4.  Qwilfish is available **only through the Super Rod**.
5.  The later tables are levelled to the project’s UU stage, with the
    highest Super Rod encounters reaching the UU cap of Lv. 39.
6.  No Route 44 Surf-gated or Super Rod encounter counts toward the
    initial LC availability audit.
7.  The Old Rod table remains available during the first visit and
    retains Dratini at 5%.

# v4.4 design notes

- Route 104 is no longer used as a catch-all for displaced Route 44
  species.
- Route 46 carries several rare rocky/side-route rewards, which is
  intentional because it is off the primary path.
- Dark Cave now has only a token 5% Geodude presence; its identity is
  driven more by Zubat, Onix, Aron, Baltoy, Magnemite, and its rare
  time-gated species.
- Route 103 now has the strongest Hoenn-backtracking identity: Voltorb
  and Wailmer are both available there without making the tiny Route 104
  patch overloaded.
- Route 44’s only signature pre-Surf catch is Dratini at 5%.

# v4.4 Route 44 canonical reference notes

The Route 44 redesign uses the Generation II tables as its ecological
reference rather than copying them literally.

- Gold/Silver grass: Weepinbell 35%, Tangela 30%, Bellsprout 20%,
  Lickitung 15%.
- Crystal morning/day: Lickitung 40%, Tangela 30%, Bellsprout 20%,
  Weepinbell 10%.
- Crystal night: Tangela 30%, Poliwag 30%, Bellsprout 20%, Weepinbell
  10%, Poliwhirl 10%.
- Canonical Surf: Poliwag 90%, Poliwhirl 10%.
- Canonical fishing strongly emphasizes Remoraid, including a 100% Super
  Rod Remoraid encounter during the Gen II Remoraid configuration/swarm.

Plastic Ox preserves that recognizable Route 44 identity while adapting
species rates and levels to its tier-gated progression.
